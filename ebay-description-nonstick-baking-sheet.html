Why Choose This Baking Sheet?

🔥

Heavy-Gauge Carbon Steel

The 0.7 mm thick carbon steel body distributes heat faster and more evenly than thin aluminium pans. No warping, no buckling — even at 450°F. A rolled rim adds rigidity and prevents oven-rack sliding.

✨

Dual-Layer PTFE Non-Stick

Two layers of PTFE (Teflon-style) non-stick coating release cookies, roasted vegetables, and fish fillets with zero sticking and minimal oil needed. Rated PFOA-free and food safe to 450°F (232°C).

🍳

Dishwasher Safe — Easy Clean

The non-stick surface wipes clean in seconds with a soapy cloth, and the coating holds up through hundreds of dishwasher cycles without flaking or peeling. Less time scrubbing, more time baking.

Feature 01

Reinforced Rolled Rim Prevents Warping

The reinforced rolled edge around all four sides stiffens the pan against high-temperature warping. Standard flat pans buckle and curl when heated above 400°F — this pan stays flat on the oven rack, keeping biscuits, cookies, and sheet cakes level from edge to edge.

  • 0.7 mm heavy-gauge carbon steel body
  • Rolled rim adds structural rigidity
  • Stays flat from 350°F to 450°F
  • Non-slip rack grip from reinforced rim
Feature 02

4 Sizes — Quarter to Full Sheet Pan

Choose the exact size for your oven and recipe: a Quarter Sheet (9”×13”) for small-batch baking, a Half Sheet (13”×18”) for everyday use, or a Full Sheet (18”×26”) for roasting large batches. Combo sets include two or three sizes for complete kitchen coverage.

  • Quarter Sheet: 9” × 13” • small batch ideal
  • Half Sheet: 13” × 18” • everyday baker’s size
  • Full Sheet: 18” × 26” • high-volume roasting
  • Sets include cooling rack option
Feature 03

Sheet + Rack Combos for Air-Circulation Roasting

Select a “Sheet + Rack” bundle for the best of both worlds: the baking sheet catches drips while the stainless-steel wire rack elevates food for 360° air circulation. Perfect for crispy bacon, roasted chicken wings, and glazed pork tenderloin without flipping.

  • Stainless-steel wire rack included in bundle
  • 360° air circulation for crispier results
  • Sheet catches drips — oven stays clean
  • Rack is oven and dishwasher safe
