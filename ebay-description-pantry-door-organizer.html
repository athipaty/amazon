Why Choose This Pantry Organizer?

🔧

Zero-Drill Installation

Two over-door hooks slide over any door up to 1.8” thick — standard or hollow core. No drilling, no wall damage. Installed or removed in under 60 seconds with no tools required.

🔐

Heavy-Duty Steel — 66 lb Capacity

Cold-rolled steel frame with powder-coat finish supports up to 30 kg (66 lbs) distributed across shelves. Each shelf holds bottles, cans, containers, or cleaning supplies without bowing.

Adjustable Shelf Spacing

Shelves slot into one of six height positions in 2” increments, letting you fit tall bottles on lower shelves and spice jars up top. Reconfigure in seconds as your pantry needs change.

Feature 01

Fits Standard & Hollow-Core Doors Up to 1.8” Thick

The reinforced over-door hook bracket is designed for doors 1.1”–1.8” thick, covering virtually every standard residential door including hollow-core, solid wood, and MDF. A protective rubber pad on the hook prevents scratching or marking the door finish.

  • Door thickness range: 1.1” to 1.8”
  • Rubber pad protects door surface
  • Stable — does not swing or rock when loaded
  • Works on pantry, kitchen, bathroom, and utility doors
Feature 02

4, 6, or 8 Shelves — Scale to Your Space

The 4-tier fits low doors or pantry alcoves with limited overhead clearance (total height 41”). The 6-tier (52”) suits most standard pantry doors. The 8-tier (63”) maximises a full-height door and stores up to 64 standard spice jars, 20 cans, or 12 full-size cereal boxes.

  • 4-tier: 41” tall • fits low clearance doors
  • 6-tier: 52” tall • most pantry doors
  • 8-tier: 63” tall • maximum storage capacity
  • All tiers: 16” wide • 5” deep per shelf
Feature 03

Powder-Coated Finish — Black or White

Both colour options use a thick powder-coat finish over cold-rolled steel, making the rack resistant to moisture, rust, and chipping. The Matte Black blends into dark pantries and modern kitchens; the White suits bright kitchens and laundry rooms.

  • Matte Black: sleek modern look, hides smudges
  • White: bright and airy, suits any style
  • Rust-resistant powder-coat over steel
  • Wipes clean with a damp cloth
