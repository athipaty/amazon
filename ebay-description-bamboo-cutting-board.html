Why Choose This Cutting Board?

🌿

Premium Moso Bamboo

Harvested from fast-growing Moso bamboo, this board is 16% harder than maple. The smooth closed-grain surface resists knife scarring and stays hygienic wash after wash.

💧

Deep Juice Groove

A 0.5” deep perimeter channel catches meat juices, fruit syrups, and vegetable liquids before they reach your countertop. Keeps your workspace clean and safe.

✅

BPA-Free & Food Safe

Made with food-safe glue and zero harmful chemicals. Naturally antibacterial bamboo inhibits odour and bacteria, making it safer than plastic boards for everyday use.

Feature 01

Deep Juice Groove on All Four Sides

The 0.5” routed perimeter channel runs along all four edges, giving you more usable surface area than boards with only one grooved side. Handles large roasts, whole chickens, and watermelons without a mess.

  • 0.5” deep groove on all 4 sides
  • Catches liquid before it reaches the counter
  • Works with meat, fruit, and vegetables
  • Flip to smooth side for bread and pastry
Feature 02

Natural vs Carbonized — Two Finish Options

Choose the warm honey tone of Natural bamboo or the rich dark espresso look of Carbonized bamboo. Both finishes use the same grade of Moso bamboo and offer identical performance — it’s purely a matter of kitchen style.

  • Natural: warm golden-blonde tone
  • Carbonized: rich dark espresso colour
  • Same hardness and juice groove depth
  • Both finished with food-safe mineral oil
Feature 03

Easy Care — Lasts for Years

Hand wash with warm soapy water and towel-dry promptly. Re-oil monthly with any food-grade mineral oil to maintain moisture and prevent cracking. Bamboo’s natural density means it won’t absorb odours like softer wood boards.

  • Hand wash only — wipe dry immediately
  • Re-oil monthly with mineral oil
  • Does not harbour odours
  • Non-slip rubber feet keep it stable
